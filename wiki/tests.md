## Protocols

**Single-leg heel raise to fatigue** (first run: baseline, 7 Oct 2026). Source:
Silbernagel-style test, `raw/research/01-achilles-and-endurance-structure.md` **[W]**; home
version, so height is approximate.
- When: morning, fresh. ≥72h after heavy calf work, no run beforehand. Same conditions every
  retest, or it's a new test.
- Set-up: barefoot, ball of foot on a step edge or a ~10° wedge if available (flat floor
  otherwise, and record which). Fingertips on a wall for balance only. Phone filming from the
  side with a tape measure or marked card behind the heel.
- Pace: metronome 60 bpm, one beat up, one beat down (30 raises/min). Knee straight, rise as
  high as possible every rep.
- Stop when: you can't keep pace, the knee bends, you lean on the wall, or height drops
  clearly (about half the early height).
- Record per leg: reps; peak height from the first reps and height at the end (from video);
  any Achilles pain during (0–10). Work ≈ reps × average height × bodyweight.
- Symmetry: weaker ÷ stronger, on work first, then height, then reps. Target ≥80–90%.
- Weigh in standard conditions the same morning (after waking, before food, minimal clothes).
