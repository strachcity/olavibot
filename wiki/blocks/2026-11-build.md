## What would change the plan
- Achilles morning pain back above 2/10 on two days, or any pain over 5/10: hold volume, drop
  that week's quality session, keep the calf work (`training-model.md` §7).
- Foot pain on walking: no quality session or jumps until it's clear.
- Illness: easy running only.
- Christmas or work squeezing week 5: tests move, the block doesn't stretch to fit them.

*Block weak points*
- Strides, jumps and a weekly quality session all arrive in one block. Staggered across the
  week, but it's still three new kinds of faster loading.
- Progression and tempo runs rest on practitioner convention, not research **[H]**.
- Tests sit one week before Christmas; if December gets busy, they're what's at risk.
