**Read the sourcing caveat in `wiki/references/evidence-norwegian-method.md` before using any
of this for planning.** In short: almost none of it is independently verified research. It's
one man's 25-year account of his own training and coaching, plus a community guide built from
a public forum thread. Two real, checkable citations appear inside it — Kjøsen Talsnes et al.
2024 (Frontiers in Physiology) and Casado & Bakken 2023 (a review Bakken co-authored, PubMed
36900796) — everything else is anecdote, however detailed.

**The elephant in the room:** Bakken's central proof-of-success chain runs through the
Ingebrigtsen family. In the 2025 article, he quotes Gjert Ingebrigtsen's sworn court testimony
that the training system is *"mine and only mine... no one has influenced it, internally or
externally"* — a direct denial of what Bakken claims throughout. Bakken disputes this and
supplies his own evidence (correspondence excerpts, a training week he says he supplied in
2006). Neither side's account is independently verified here. Treat every claim of the form
"and that's why the Ingebrigtsens are so good" as contested, not established.
