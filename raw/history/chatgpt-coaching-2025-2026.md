## Thu 30 Jul 2026, 03:07 — Jack

> Whats the bone on back of heel near achillies, thats quite big compared to the other

### ChatGPT's response

The bone you're almost certainly feeling is the **calcaneus** (heel bone). Your Achilles
tendon attaches to the back of the calcaneus, so it's completely normal to be able to feel a
prominent bony area there.

Possibilities if one side genuinely feels bigger:
1. Normal anatomical variation.
2. Swelling around the tendon or bursa after an injury.
3. A bony prominence (**Haglund's deformity**), sometimes called a "pump bump", where the upper
   back of the heel bone is naturally more prominent. This can irritate the Achilles or the
   bursa in some runners.

Given your morning pain has resolved, you've tolerated both a run and a gym session, and you're
walking normally, I'm actually less concerned that you've developed a serious insertional
Achilles problem. **Your symptoms have mainly been 2–6 cm above the heel, which fits more with
the mid-portion of the tendon than the point where it inserts into the bone.**

A couple of questions will help narrow it down:
- Is the "big" bit hard like bone, or does it feel soft or squishy?
- Is it visible compared with the other side, or does it only feel bigger when you touch it?
- Is it directly where the tendon inserts into the heel, or slightly above it?

If you're able to, take a photo of both heels from behind and from the side while standing.
